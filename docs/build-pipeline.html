Inside Knowmler

Knowmler starts with the source.

A user brings in a video, PDF, or article. Knowmler summarizes it, pulls out the main ideas, and shows where there may be a useful tool to build.

The app idea is not a blank prompt. It comes from the analysis: what the content is about, who it helps, and what workflow would make the next step easier.

Prototype review

The prototype proves whether the idea is worth building.

Knowmler makes a small clickable version first. People can look at it, comment, vote, and decide whether it should become a real app.

If the idea is promoted, Foundry gets a clearer brief: the source analysis, the app idea, the prototype, and the feedback that made it worth building.

Where Foundry enters

Context Foundry builds the promoted idea.

Foundry receives the brief from Knowmler and builds the app. It handles the implementation work, checks the result, and publishes a preview.

Knowmler decides what is worth building.

It keeps the analysis, idea, prototype, and promotion signal together.

Foundry does the build.

It turns the promoted idea into a hosted preview people can use.

Back to the user

The live preview comes back to Knowmler.

When the build is ready, Knowmler shows the app link beside the promoted idea. People can open the preview, compare it with the prototype, and keep working from the same place.

The flow stays simple: Knowmler finds and validates the idea. Context Foundry builds it. Knowmler shows the result.

Use Knowmler to turn content into a clear app idea. Use Context Foundry to turn the promoted idea into a live preview.
